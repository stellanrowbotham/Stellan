# Memex
[[vannevar-bush]]'s 1945 vision of a personal, curated knowledge store connected by associative trails between documents.

## Explanation
The Memex was a private, actively curated store in which the **connections between documents mattered as much as the documents themselves** ([[src-karpathy-llm-wiki]]). Karpathy argues that Bush's vision is closer to the [[llm-wiki]] than to the web that came later. The web is public and loosely curated. The Memex was private and deliberately linked ([[src-karpathy-llm-wiki]]).

The part Bush could not solve was **who does the maintenance**. In the LLM Wiki pattern, the LLM does ([[src-karpathy-llm-wiki]]). See [[maintenance-burden]].
