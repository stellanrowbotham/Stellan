# Daily Run-Through
Stellan's daily routine with Claude: a **morning check-in** to plan the day and an **end-of-day review** that files the day into the brain *(Stellan, 2026-10-10)*.

## How it works
1. **Morning:** say "morning" or "gm". Claude asks the morning questions below, saves your answers word for word in `raw/daily/`, writes today's page, and replies with a day plan.
2. **Evening:** say "eod" or "wrap up". Claude asks the evening questions, ticks off the morning's Top 3, and files each update into [[flowpilot]] or [[investing-apprentice]].
3. Anything unfinished carries over to **Open loops** below, and Claude raises it the next morning.
4. Each check-in also asks one **open question** from a hub, so the brain fills in over time without a big interview.

## Morning questions
1. **Energy:** 1–10, and how did you sleep?
2. **Top 3:** the three things that would make today a win.
3. **FlowPilot:** any client work, calls, deadlines, or builds today?
4. **Apprentice:** what are you building, testing, or researching on the product today?
5. **On your mind:** anything else, such as worries, ideas, or appointments?

## Evening questions
1. **Top 3 check:** what got done, and what didn't?
2. **FlowPilot:** wins, new leads, client updates, money in, automations shipped?
3. **Apprentice:** progress, bugs, decisions made, market notes?
4. **Learned / ideas:** anything worth keeping?
5. **Tomorrow:** the first task, and your energy now (1–10).
