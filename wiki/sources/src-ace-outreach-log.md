# ACE — Contacted Businesses Log (v8)
> **Raw:** a Google Doc owned by Stellan. A structured copy without email addresses is kept in the app's private database (`ace/outreach`), not in this public repo · **Author:** [[ace]] · **Updated:** 2026-09-18 · **Ingested:** 2026-10-10

## TL;DR
This is [[ace]]'s running log of every business it has cold-emailed for [[flowpilot]]. It lists 69 local businesses in southwestern Ontario across 4 batches, sent 2026-09-13 to 2026-09-15. The log exists so that no business is ever contacted twice.

## Key points
- **Rule:** one outreach email per business, ever. Check the log before adding anyone to a new batch.
- **Batches:** 1 = 20 businesses (2026-09-13), 2 = 30 (2026-09-14), 3 = 8 (2026-09-15), 4 = 11 (2026-09-15). Stellan confirmed each batch as sent.
- **Industries** (sorted by Claude from the business names): real estate 8, dental 6, hair salons 6, bakeries 5, fitness 5, landscaping 5, pet grooming 5, auto repair 4, accounting 4, cleaning 4, restaurants 4, florists 3, retail 3, photography 2, cafés 2, law 1, chiropractic 1, other professional services 1.
- **Towns:** 16, including Cambridge, Guelph, Kitchener, Waterloo, London, Brantford, Woodstock, Stratford, Sarnia, St. Thomas, Tillsonburg, Simcoe, Chatham, Ingersoll, Elora and Fergus.
- **Batches 3–4:** every email address was taken from the business's own website, Facebook page or official listing, and cross-checked.
- **Statuses:** Drafted, Sent, or Replied. Replies are handled by Stellan personally, not by ACE.
- v8 replaces v6 and all earlier log docs.
