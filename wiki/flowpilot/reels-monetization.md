# Making money from the reels
Ways the daily [[instagram-reels]] can earn money, ranked by how likely they are to pay soon.

## Summary
The reels will most likely pay through **FlowPilot itself**: as marketing that brings in automation clients, and as a sellable service ("animated AI reels with voiceover for your business"). Instagram's own payouts are small, invite-only, or need 10K followers. They are a bonus, not a plan. *(synthesis)*

## Options, best first
1. **Leads for FlowPilot.** Every reel ends with a DM keyword ("PLAN", "BOOK", "CALL"). One client for an automation build is likely worth more than months of creator payouts. Track DMs per reel in `reels/posted.md`. *(synthesis)*
2. **Sell the reel machine as a FlowPilot offer.** The same pipeline (code animation, ElevenLabs voices, scheduled posting) can make reels for a salon, plumber or bakery. "Create social media posts, reels, and marketing content for businesses" shows up on 2026 lists of AI businesses to start ([Instagram list](https://www.instagram.com/p/De3FSoVEtYm/)). Price: *(not set; Stellan to decide)*.
3. **Affiliate links** in the bio or captions for tools the reels use (for example voice or automation tools), where an affiliate program exists. *(general knowledge; check each program's terms)*
4. **Instagram's own programs.** Gifts on reels need 500+ followers ([WERSM, 2026](https://wersm.com/how-social-platforms-pay-creators-2026/)). Subscriptions need about 10K followers and age 18+ ([Buffer, 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/how-to-make-money-on-instagram/)). Bonuses are invite-only, with reported rates of about $0.01–0.05 per 1,000 views ([Buffer, 2026](https://buffer.com/resources/how-to-make-money-on-instagram/)).
5. **Cross-posting** to TikTok and YouTube Shorts for reach. TikTok's Creator Rewards needs 10,000 followers, 100,000 views in 30 days, and videos over 1 minute ([WERSM, 2026](https://wersm.com/how-social-platforms-pay-creators-2026/)).
6. **Brand deals** later, once there's a following. Many creators land first deals at 1,000–5,000 engaged followers ([Hootsuite, 2026](https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-monetization/)).

## Tensions & contradictions
- ⚠️ Most platform payouts require the account holder to be **18 or older**. Status: open.
- ⚠️ Instagram's original-content rules: reels must be original, which ours are (code animation and our own scripts). Reposting with other apps' watermarks can disqualify them ([Hootsuite, 2026](https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-monetization/)).
